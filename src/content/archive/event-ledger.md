This page tracks known BREAD events and open leads. Rows are sorted by date.

## How to read this table

- **Date**: Event date when confirmed from a listing or flyer.
- **Series**: Party number or special program name.
- **Venue/platform**: Where it happened, in person or online.
- **Lineup highlights**: Main names shown in currently available sources.
- **Main source**: Best currently linked source.
- **Status**: `strong` means a dated public listing or flyer was found; `archive-sourced` marks a record retained from the archive that may be refined as more sources are linked.
