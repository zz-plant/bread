## The setting

In the mid-2010s, San Francisco nightlife was shifting under rapid economic and cultural pressure. BREAD formed to create space for less-safe, forward-leaning club music and community-centered programming.

## The arc

- **Formation and pilot (2014–2015):** initial organizing, early F8 events, and first audience signals.
- **Expansion period (2016):** residency momentum, larger guest bookings, and stronger curatorial identity.
- **Bunker period (2017–2018):** venue shifts and broader rhythmic palette during a tighter local climate.
- **Mutual-aid streams (2021):** online fundraising programming during pandemic-era shutdowns.
