This site is organized as a long-living archive for BREAD's history, not just a single story post.

## How this archive is structured

- **Timeline**: chronology and era framing. See [Timeline & Eras](/archive/timeline/).
- **Event ledger**: row-level records with source-basis columns. See [Event Ledger (Working)](/archive/event-ledger/).
- **People & Network**: founders, residents, collaborators, and scene effects. See [People & Network](/archive/people-network/).
- **Venues & Format Shifts**: venue phases and format evolution. See [Venues & Format Shifts](/archive/venues-format-shifts/).
- **Methods & confidence**: evidence model and verification limits. See [Research Methods & Source Confidence](/archive/research-methods-confidence/).
- **Channel directory**: canonical links and discovered event URLs. See [Channel & Profile Directory](/archive/channel-profile-directory/).
- **Narrative version**: story-form onboarding. See [The Story of BREAD](/blog/the-story-of-bread/).

## Archival principles

- Preserve claims with clear dates where possible.
- Separate **confirmed** facts from **pending verification** notes.
- Keep pages linkable and updatable as new primary evidence appears.

## Current source status

The archive currently incorporates the in-repo research brief, user-supplied canonical draft notes, and link-discovery passes. As primary artifacts are attached event-by-event, confidence labels can be upgraded from directional to verified documentation.
