BREAD was a San Francisco club night from 2015 to 2018, with a radio show, BREAD FM, alongside it. In 2021 it came back for three fundraiser streams, and from 2022 BREAD FM was on the air again. This archive collects the flyers, lineups, and links that are left.

## What's here

- [Timeline](/archive/timeline/): the run in five stretches, with key dates.
- [Event list](/archive/event-ledger/): every known night, with lineups and sources.
- [People](/archive/people-network/): founders, residents, and collaborators.
- [Venue map](/archive/map/): the rooms, from F8 to Underground SF, and what happened in each.
- [Flyers](/archive/flyers/) and [Radio shows](/archive/radio-shows/): the artwork for the nights and for BREAD FM, plus links to BREAD's channels.
- [The Story of BREAD](/story/): the whole run in one read.

## Where it comes from

Dates and lineups come from flyers, public listings, and social posts, plus BREAD's own records. When a source doesn't give an exact venue or lineup, the entry says so instead of guessing. The archive gets updated as more material turns up.
