## Founders

BREAD was started by **Cage A** (Kanav) and **Prawns**, with **Judy (dhra)** as co-founder. They wanted a San Francisco night for riskier, less commercial club music than the city usually booked.

## Residents

The residents played nearly every night, and the group changed as BREAD did.

- **Botaz** and **Gewzer** joined in 2015. Both are on the flyer for BREAD #3 that November. Botaz also did most of BREAD's graphic design.
- **Adam Kraft** and **TF1000 / Tranespotting** joined in 2016.
- **Misoh** was a resident until about 2018, when the club nights ended.
- **Exteeng** joined in 2018 and **Shl** in 2021, so most of their time with BREAD was on the streams and the radio show.

Some people use different names on different platforms, so the same person can show up under more than one handle. Profile links are on the [Radio shows](/archive/radio-shows/) page.

## Guests

Most nights were built around guests from outside the city, and the flyers print the labels they came from: Night Slugs, Planet Mu, Tectonic, Trouble & Bass, Purple Tape Pedigree. Partner nights were shared with Grime City, Nightsweats, Parameter, and Sure Thing. Every guest is listed night by night in the [Event list](/archive/event-ledger/).

## After the club nights

By the time BREAD came back in 2021, the residents no longer lived in one place. The flyer for the New Year stream tags them with Oakland, San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Tokyo. The fundraiser streams, and then BREAD FM's monthly show, were how the group kept playing together.

This page sticks to what people did with BREAD. The lists come from BREAD's own records and may change as more turns up.
