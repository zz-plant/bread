## Era 1 — Formation & Pilot (2014–2015)

- **2014:** public profiles describe the project as active since 2014.
- **2015-02-13:** pilot event at F8 with Helix.
- **2015-05 onward:** the launch period and recurring events in SF.

## Era 2 — Growing the Nights (2015–2016)

- A stronger resident crew and clearer visual style.
- Guest bookings brought in artists connected to UK/US club crossover scenes.
- A 2015 screenshot references a May 29 SF event, confirming public activity by mid-2015.
- **2016:** growth-year cluster includes Jlin, Distal, Celestial Trax, Deadboy, Murlo, Rabit, Logos, Mumdance, and a run of partner showcases (Parameter, Sure Thing, Toasted Trax).

## Era 3 — Underground SF Years (2017–2018)

- More nights moved toward Underground SF as the city scene shifted.
- The sound opened up toward warmer, globally rooted rhythms.
- Timeline materials include Mr. Mitch + Kozee and Kelman Duran periods, with 2018 marking final club-night phase.

## Era 4 — Remote Fundraisers + Radio (2020–2023)

- **2020–2021:** livestream/telethon format focused on fundraising.
- Screenshot materials and archived notes align on approximately **USD 12K** raised across multiple streams.
- **2021-01-02:** New Year telethon with multi-time-zone schedule and no-cover framing.
- **2021-03-13:** Six Years stream appears as anniversary fundraiser period.
- **2021-07-10:** "Long Bread Summer" stream staged as a fundraiser for Rebuilding Alliance and Oxygen for India.
- **2022–2023:** public traces show BREAD FM activity through Internet Public Radio and HKCR.
