## Primary venue phases

### F8 period (early cycle)

F8 appears as an early anchor for pilot and resident activity, especially in the first growth years.

### Underground SF period (later club cycle)

Programming emphasis later shifted to Underground SF as city conditions and scene dynamics changed.

## Additional venue references in research notes

Archive notes also mention Public Works Loft, Mighty, Bruno's, Codeword, Club 6ix, and OMG.

These are best tracked as event-linked venue records, not one-off mentions.

## Format transitions

- **Club-night format:** recurring in-person events with residents + guests.
- **Radio format:** BREAD FM continuity between parties and later online programming.
- **Telethon format:** pandemic-era livestream fundraising.
